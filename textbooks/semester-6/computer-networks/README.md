## 🎯 Module Mapping Summary

- **Module I (Network Architecture & Reference Models)**: Primary reference: Tanenbaum (Chapters 1 & 2).
- **Module II (Data Link Layer & MAC Sublayer)**: Primary references: Tanenbaum (Chapters 3 & 4), Forouzan (Chapters 11, 12 & 13).
- **Module III (Network Layer & Routing Algorithms)**: Primary references: Tanenbaum (Chapter 5), Peterson & Davie (Chapter 3).
- **Module IV (Congestion Control, QoS & IPv4 Subnetting)**: Primary references: Forouzan (Chapters 18 & 19), Peterson & Davie (Chapter 5).
- **Module V (Internet Control Protocols, Multicasting & IPv6)**: Primary references: Forouzan (Chapters 20 & 22), Peterson & Davie (Chapter 4).
- **Module VI (Transport Layer & Application Protocols)**: Primary references: Tanenbaum (Chapters 6 & 7), Peterson & Davie (Chapters 5 & 9).
