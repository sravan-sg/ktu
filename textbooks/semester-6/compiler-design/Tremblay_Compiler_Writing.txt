Title: The Theory and Practice of Compiler Writing
Author: Jean-Paul Tremblay and Paul G. Sorenson
Edition: Tata McGraw-Hill, 1984

Chapter 1: Compiler Overview
Discusses the compilation process and fundamental translator concepts.

Chapter 2: Finite State Machines
Introduction to NFAs and DFAs for token recognition and lexical analysis.

Chapter 3: Grammar and Language Theory
Formal languages and syntax-directed definitions.

Chapter 4: Parsing Techniques
In-depth look at LR(k) parsing and precedence parsing strategies.

Chapter 5: Code Optimization and Generation
Storage allocation, basic block identification, and register allocation.
