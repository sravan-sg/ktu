## Module Coverage Mapping

| Module | Primary Textbook / Resource | Specific Sections Covered |
|---|---|---|
| **Module I** | Donald Hearn & M. Pauline Baker / MRCET Notes | Display devices, CRT architecture, Raster-scan & Vector-scan refresh, interactive input devices. |
| **Module II** | Donald Hearn & M. Pauline Baker / Rogers | DDA line algorithm, Bresenham's integer line algorithm, Midpoint circle algorithm, Scan-line polygon fill, Boundary-fill, Flood-fill. |
| **Module III** | Donald Hearn & M. Pauline Baker / Xiang & Plastock | 2D affine transformations, homogeneous coordinates ($3 \times 3$), composite transformations, Window-to-Viewport mapping, Cohen-Sutherland line clipping, Midpoint subdivision. |
| **Module IV** | Donald Hearn & M. Pauline Baker | Sutherland-Hodgeman polygon clipping, Weiler-Atherton clipping, Polygon & Quadric 3D representations, 3D translation/rotation/scaling matrices. |
| **Module V** | Donald Hearn & M. Pauline Baker / Rogers | Parallel (orthographic & oblique) and Perspective projections, vanishing points, Back-face removal, Z-buffer, A-buffer, Depth-sorting (Painter's), Scan-line visible surface detection. |
| **Module VI** | Rafael C. Gonzalez & Richard E. Woods / Gose et al. | Digital image representation, pixel relationships (neighbors, $m$-adjacency, distance metrics $D_e, D_4, D_8$), histograms, spatial convolution/correlation, edge operators (Roberts, Prewitt, Sobel, Canny). |
