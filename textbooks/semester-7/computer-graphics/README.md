## Notes

- **Hearn & Baker** is the primary textbook covering all algorithmic content (Modules I–V). The Internet Archive has a borrowable digital copy; for offline access the book is widely available in university libraries.
- **Gose, Johnsonbaugh & Jost** covers Module VI (image processing fundamentals, edge detection, region labeling). This is a specialist text that requires library/institutional access.
- **Gonzalez & Woods** *Digital Image Processing* (Pearson, 2017) is an excellent freely available supplementary resource for Module VI and is preferred by many instructors over Gose et al. for its more detailed treatment of convolution, histogram operations, and edge detection.
