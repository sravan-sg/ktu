## Module Summary

Module 2 covers **power supply regulation** and **field effect transistors**:

- **Zener regulator**: Simplest, limited current capacity
- **Series regulator**: Efficient, high current via error amplifier + pass transistor
- **78XX**: Complete IC regulators (7805, 7812 etc.) for plug-and-play voltage regulation
- **IC 723**: Flexible regulator — configurable for low voltage (<7.15V) and high voltage (>7.15V)
- **SMPS**: Switching regulator — 85-90% efficiency, compact for computer PSUs
- **JFET**: Voltage-controlled, high input impedance, no gate current
- **MOSFET**: Enhancement (off by default, VGS turns on channel) and Depletion (on by default)
