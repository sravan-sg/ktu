## Module Summary

Module 3 is the amplifier module — the core of analog electronics:

- **Q-point/load line**: Biasing sets up the DC operating point on the load line
- **Voltage divider bias**: Most stable (β-independent), universal choice
- **Thermal runaway**: Prevented by emitter resistance RE and proper stability factor
- **RC coupled amplifier**: AC-coupled stages, mid-band gain Av = -gm×RC', lower/upper cutoff due to coupling/bypass capacitors
- **Cascading**: Total gain = product of individual gains; bandwidth narrows
- **Negative feedback**: Reduces gain but improves stability, bandwidth, distortion, and impedance
