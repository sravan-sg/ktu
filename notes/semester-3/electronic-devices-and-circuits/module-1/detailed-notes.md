## Module Summary

Module 1 covers **wave-shaping circuits** — the fundamental building blocks for manipulating the shape of signals using passive RC networks and diodes.

- **RC circuits** perform mathematical operations (differentiation and integration) on waveforms
- **Clippers** limit signal amplitude (used in protection and waveform squaring)
- **Clampers** shift signal DC level (used in TV sync and voltage multiplication)
- **Voltage multipliers** generate high DC voltages from low AC sources without transformers
- **Sweep circuits** generate linear ramps for oscilloscope and CRT display timebases
