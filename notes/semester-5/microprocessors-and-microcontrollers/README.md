## Course Overview

This repository hosts comprehensive, research-grade, mathematically rigorous study materials for **CS305: Microprocessors and Microcontrollers**. Authored under the pedagogical stance of a Senior Computer Science Professor, every topic features fundamental theoretical intuition, architectural hardware block diagrams, industrial application engineering use-cases, at least 3 step-by-step solved analytical/numerical walkthroughs, and 100% complete, self-contained Previous Year Question (PYQ) solutions.
