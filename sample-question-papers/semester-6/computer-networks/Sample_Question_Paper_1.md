PART A
Answer all questions. Each question carries 3 marks.
1. Differentiate between Hub, Bridge, and Switch.
2. Explain the purpose of framing in the Data Link Layer.
3. What is the role of ARP in a local network?
4. Mention any three fields of the IPv4 packet header.

PART B
Answer any two full questions. Each question carries 9 marks.
5. a) Explain the OSI Reference Model with a neat diagram. (5)
   b) Compare TCP/IP and OSI models. (4)
6. a) Explain CSMA/CD protocol. How does it detect collisions? (6)
   b) What is the hidden node problem in wireless LANs? (3)
7. a) Describe the working of Distance Vector Routing. (6)
   b) What is the count-to-infinity problem? (3)

PART C
Answer all questions. Each question carries 3 marks.
8. Explain the difference between Flow Control and Congestion Control.
9. What is a three-way handshake in TCP?
10. Describe the significance of the Subnet Mask.
11. How does BGP prevent routing loops?

PART D
Answer any two full questions. Each question carries 9 marks.
12. a) Explain TCP Congestion Control mechanisms (AIMD, Slow Start). (6)
    b) What is Fast Retransmit? (3)
13. a) Explain Leaky Bucket and Token Bucket algorithms for Traffic Shaping. (6)
    b) Differentiate between TCP and UDP. (3)
14. a) Describe the structure of an IPv6 address and its advantages over IPv4. (6)
    b) Explain any one IPv4-to-IPv6 transition strategy. (3)

PART E
Answer any four questions. Each question carries 10 marks.
15. Explain Link State Routing (OSPF) with an example topology.
16. Discuss various techniques for improving Quality of Service (QoS).
17. Explain the function of DNS. Describe the iterative and recursive resolution process.
18. Detail the operation of SMTP and IMAP for email delivery.
19. Explain HTTP protocol. Differentiate between persistent and non-persistent connections.
20. Discuss the role of ICMP and explain any three ICMP error messages.
