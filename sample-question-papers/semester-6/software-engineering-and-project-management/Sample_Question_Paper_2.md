# Sample Question Paper 2 (KTU Pattern)
## CS308 — Software Engineering and Project Management

### PART A (Answer all questions, 4 x 3 = 12 marks)
1. How does prototyping help in software development? (3)
2. What is the scope of software engineering? (3)
3. Explain the importance of requirements. How many types of requirements are possible? (3)
4. List the phases in software development requirement analysis. (3)

### PART B (Answer any two full questions, 2 x 9 = 18 marks)
5. (a) Explain the incremental process model with its advantages. (5)
   (b) Describe ISO 9000 certification and its significance in software development. (4)
6. (a) Explain software prototyping in detail. (5)
   (b) Discuss the historical and economic aspects of software engineering. (4)
7. (a) Explain the processes, methods, and tools layer of software engineering. (5)
   (b) Detail the requirement elicitation techniques. (4)

### PART C (Answer all questions, 4 x 3 = 12 marks)
8. What is stepwise refinement? (3)
9. Define software scope. (3)
10. What are size measures in coding? (3)
11. Briefly explain the concept of basis path testing. (3)

### PART D (Answer any two full questions, 2 x 9 = 18 marks)
12. (a) Describe staffing and personal planning. (5)
    (b) Explain the concepts of the design process and design principles. (4)
13. (a) Explain complexity analysis in coding. (5)
    (b) Discuss control structure testing. (4)
14. (a) Compare the COCOMO model with single variable models. (5)
    (b) Differentiate between validation testing and system testing. (4)

### PART E (Answer any four full questions, 4 x 10 = 40 marks)
15. (a) Explain software risks and risk management. (5)
    (b) Discuss the taxonomy of CASE tools. (5)
16. (a) How is a task set selected for a software project? (5)
    (b) Detail the concept of Project Management in terms of People, Product, Process, and Project. (5)
17. (a) Explain the basics and standards of Software Configuration Management (SCM). (5)
    (b) What are the types of maintenance? (5)
18. (a) Discuss CASE building blocks and the integrated CASE environment. (5)
    (b) Explain the basic concepts of project scheduling and tracking. (5)
19. (a) What are the golden rules for user interface design? (5)
    (b) Describe risk identification and monitoring. (5)
20. (a) Explain the maintenance process overview. (5)
    (b) How is the relationship between people and effort established? (5)
