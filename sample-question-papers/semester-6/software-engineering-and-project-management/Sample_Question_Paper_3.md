# Sample Question Paper 3 (KTU Pattern)
## CS308 — Software Engineering and Project Management

### PART A (Answer all questions, 4 x 3 = 12 marks)
1. Contrast the CMM and ISO 9000 process frameworks. (3)
2. Explain the team programming aspects of software engineering. (3)
3. What is a specification document? (3)
4. State the primary differences between the Waterfall and Spiral process models. (3)

### PART B (Answer any two full questions, 2 x 9 = 18 marks)
5. (a) Detail the waterfall model. In what situations is it most appropriate? (5)
   (b) Discuss the core principles of requirement analysis. (4)
6. (a) Explain the spiral model for software development with a neat diagram. (5)
   (b) Describe any three methods of the requirement elicitation process. (4)
7. (a) Describe the different levels of the Capability Maturity Model. (5)
   (b) Differentiate between the waterfall model and the incremental model for software development. (4)

### PART C (Answer all questions, 4 x 3 = 12 marks)
8. What is the purpose of empirical estimation models? (3)
9. Differentiate between Unit Testing and Integration Testing. (3)
10. What is the significance of coding standards? (3)
11. What is the objective of project planning? (3)

### PART D (Answer any two full questions, 2 x 9 = 18 marks)
12. (a) Elaborate on the COCOMO model for software estimation. (5)
    (b) Discuss effective modular design principles. (4)
13. (a) Explain White Box testing and its techniques. (5)
    (b) What are code inspections and walk-throughs? (4)
14. (a) Discuss top-down and bottom-up design strategies. (5)
    (b) Explain Basis Path Testing with a suitable example. (4)

### PART E (Answer any four full questions, 4 x 10 = 40 marks)
15. (a) Discuss the types of software maintenance. (5)
    (b) Explain the basic concepts of project scheduling and tracking. (5)
16. (a) Detail the 4 P's (People, Product, Process, Project) of project management. (5)
    (b) What are the rules for User Interface design? (5)
17. (a) Explain the processes involved in risk identification, monitoring, and management. (6)
    (b) Discuss the relationship between people and effort. (4)
18. (a) What is Software Configuration Management? Discuss its standards. (5)
    (b) Describe the building blocks and taxonomy of CASE tools. (5)
19. (a) Explain how to define a task set for a software project. (5)
    (b) Discuss the integrated CASE environment. (5)
20. (a) Provide an overview of the software maintenance process. (5)
    (b) How do you select a software engineering task for a project? (5)
